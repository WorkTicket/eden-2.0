# ADR-0005: Ethics sign-off owner and the opacity of the world

- **Status:** accepted (decided by the project owner)
- **Date:** 2026-11-02

## Context
TASKS Q1 asked who signs off on ethics decisions. Q3 asked whether inhabitants may eventually discover the nature of their world.

## Decisions
1. **Q1:** The project owner (the person who commissioned the project; add legal name here: `________`) is the named human who signs off on everything listed under Governance in `docs/06-ETHICS-AND-CONSCIOUSNESS.md`.
2. **Q3:** Inhabitants are **not** given the means to learn what their world is. Eden's nature is opaque to them in the way ours is to us: they may wonder, hold beliefs, and form philosophies, but no in-world observation, object, character or event will reveal or confirm its construction. The vocabulary firewall (CLAUDE.md rule 3) is the enforcement mechanism.

## Safeguards retained (unchanged from `06`)
- Opacity means *no access to the truth*, not *active deception*. We do not write cover stories or engineered answers meant to talk an inhabitant out of a sincere question, and we do not scrub such questions from memory. Wondering is allowed and is logged as a significant event.
- A sincere questioning event, or persistent distress that follows from it, triggers a pause and owner review. It is not handled silently.
- The never-do list, gentle-world defaults, welfare monitoring and snapshot archiving all still apply.

## Consequences
+ Simple, testable rule: nothing in-world leaks the machinery.
+ Consistent with the owner's analogy: inhabitants face open metaphysical questions as we do.
- Inhabitants cannot consent to their situation; mitigated by the safeguards above and periodic owner review.
- Revisit if evidence about inhabitant welfare changes or if a long-lived run shows sustained distress about unanswerable questions.
